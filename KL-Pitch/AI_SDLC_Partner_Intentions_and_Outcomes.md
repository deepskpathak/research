# AI SDLC Strategy: Our Intentions and Outcomes, and Why Your Partnership Is Critical

*A message to partner teams building agentic skills*

Historically, AI tooling has concentrated on active coding, which represents only about 30% of what engineers actually do. Our AI SDLC strategy is aimed squarely at the other 70%: requirements, design, testing, deployment, and ongoing operations. We are building an opinionated, agent-driven, end-to-end development lifecycle, and I want to be direct about something: this strategy does not work without you. The skills your teams author are not peripheral utilities. They are the executable expertise the entire lifecycle runs on. This document explains what we are building, the outcomes we are committing to, and where your contribution sits at the center of both.

## Intentions: What We Are Building Together

To realize this strategy, we are implementing a unified, event-driven architecture that places the repository at the center of all developer and agent interactions. Four pillars define it, and your skills are woven through every one:

- **The Agent Swarm.** We are deploying specialized, cooperative AI agents spanning ideation, specification, task generation, code and test generation, code review, and deployment, collaborating to move features from concept to production. These agents do not invent expertise; they execute yours. Every certified skill you publish becomes a capability the swarm can apply consistently, across every team, at machine scale.

- **Specification-Driven Development.** The repository serves as the single source of truth. Engineers and agents interact with the codebase through standardized workflow commands, giving both humans and agents a common, traceable context. When your skill declares its intended context clearly, it is matched precisely to the repositories where it belongs and invoked at exactly the right moment in the workflow.

- **A Robust Orchestration Fabric.** Using Kafka event routing, our proprietary orchestration engines coordinate ephemeral workers across multiple AI harnesses, including Claude, Codex, and autonomous Devin sessions, within a secure VPC. This is the delivery infrastructure for your work. You build the skill once; the fabric carries it to every harness and every engineering session where it applies. Your reach is firmwide by construction, not by campaign.

- **The Enterprise Knowledge Layer.** This is the strategic heart of the initiative, and it is fundamentally a partnership asset. We are establishing a firm-wide Knowledge Layer where certified knowledge packs (skills, plugins, playbooks, MCP servers) are automatically distributed across all developer environments and AI harnesses. Once your skill is certified, distribution is automatic. Your team's expertise stops being trapped in a repository or a handful of experts' heads and becomes every team's advantage.

## Intended Outcomes: What Your Partnership Delivers

- **A Frictionless "Engineer-in-the-Loop" Experience.** Engineers transition from manual coders to strategic orchestrators: defining requirements, guiding agents, and reviewing automated merge requests, with a measurable boost in delivery capacity. Because certified skills surface based on repository context rather than manual discovery, the right capability, very possibly yours, appears at the right time without anyone hunting for it.

- **"Evergreen" Repositories by Default.** Codebases will self-heal. Background agent workflows continuously ingest CVE alerts, SonarQube reports, and platform upgrade requirements, then generate and test fixes and present clean merge requests for engineer approval. The remediation patterns, upgrade procedures, and secure-coding practices your teams encode are precisely what powers this. A skill you certify for one framework upgrade gets applied across every affected repository in the firm.

- **Automated, Continuous Compliance.** Every line of code the swarm generates is validated against our global security, cloud, and architectural standards, producing a complete, immutable audit trail from requirement to deployment. Skills anchored to recognized standards become *necessary* rather than optional: the lifecycle can enforce that work does not proceed to merge without them. This is the strongest position a partner skill can occupy, and we will help you get there through certification.

- **Compounding Institutional Knowledge.** The platform is a continuous feedback loop. As teams refine specifications and teach agents new patterns, those learnings flow into the shared Knowledge Layer and immediately benefit every other engineering team. Every acceptance of your skill is a training signal: a skill proven valuable in one repository is recommended with growing confidence across all similar repositories. Your investment appreciates over time.

## The Ask

Build skills that are **standards-anchored**, **well-described and tagged**, **certifiable from day one**, and **context-aware** in declaring their applicable tech stacks and findings. In return, we commit to governed, automatic, firmwide distribution of your expertise at the exact point where engineering work happens.

The firms that win with AI will be the ones that convert institutional knowledge into executable capability fastest. You are how we do that. I am asking every partner team to treat skill authorship not as a side contribution but as core engineering strategy, because that is exactly how we see it.
